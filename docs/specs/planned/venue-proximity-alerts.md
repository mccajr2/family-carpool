## Problem

When kids (own circle and/or followed calendars) are at or near the same venue
around the same time, adults want a heads-up so they can combine trips or catch
a game they would otherwise miss.

## Non-goals (sketch)

- Live turn-by-turn / paid live traffic
- Auto-creating carpool requests from proximity alone

## Notes

  `read-only-calendar-follows` and/or circle calendar.
