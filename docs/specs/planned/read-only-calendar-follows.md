## Problem

Families sometimes want a kid’s calendar to include events they do not “own”
as a competing household activity — e.g. watching last year’s teammates on a
new team when schedules allow — without full membership in that team’s carpool
or coverage workflows.

## Non-goals (sketch)

- Making follows grant carpool/coverage rights
- Venue proximity notifications (`venue-proximity-alerts`)
- Coach/league admin feeds

## Notes

- Likely builds on activity-feed / calendar surfaces.
- Permission model: read-only overlay on the family calendar.
